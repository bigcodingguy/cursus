## Notes (2026-10-18 baseline)

- The establishment route is the most reliable way to the quaestorship. The tribal assembly is dominated by the 31 country tribes, where the boni's favor counts most. Orator and populist bots lose there because the boni dislike them.
- Orators and populists who defy their father's creditor often see the family ruined: father bankrupt in 35–39% of runs. This is the intended cost of moment 2.
- Gaius Gracchus's fall now ripens in about 90% of runs. In 121 the hard-line consul of the year leads it, whoever that is.
- The establishment row shows a few trials from being asked to defend (0.0 per run rounded).
