## Notes (polish pass, 2026-10-18)

- The three paths now reach the quaestorship in 68–86% of runs, against 56–90% before. The populist runs a little above the 75% target and the establishment a little below. Their risks differ: orators and populists defy creditors and lose land; the establishment carries more debt and loses more first attempts.
- Fathers are no longer bankrupted. A debt the land can cover now ends in a forced sale of land (27–32% of orator and populist runs).
- Stability recovers after bloodshed: political murder in 132, riots by 131, debate around 126–123. It falls again with Gaius Gracchus's laws (122) and the Aventine (121–120).
- Bot deaths are 4–6%, mostly on campaign: the bots now take every spring's offer of service.
