# Decisions

- 2026-10-17: Stack is TypeScript + Vite + React + PixiJS, static site on GitHub Pages.
- 2026-10-17: Open design questions resolved with defaults (see DESIGN.md).
- 2026-10-18: Calendar is a 365-day year with Julian month lengths and Republican month names (Quintilis, Sextilis); no intercalation. Day 0 = Kal. Ian. 133 BC; the game starts Kal. Mart.
- 2026-10-18: Systems may import another system's `api.ts` for pure queries (opinion, standing, cash); all mutation goes through bus events. Bus events emitted inside a handler are queued and run after it.
- 2026-10-18: Quaestorship rules for the slice: age 27 in the year of office, at least one campaign season served abroad. Military service is abstract (200 days away, a death risk shown up front).
- 2026-10-18: 8 quaestors a year. When the cast is short of candidates, men from houses outside the cast come forward at the declaration deadline; they become full characters and play by the same rules.
- 2026-10-18: Elections are run 200 times from a seed fixed by the election; the shown chance is the share you win, and the real vote is one of those runs picked by lot — so the odds are exact. NPC-only elections use one run.
- 2026-10-18: Trials: 32 jurors (up to 14 named from the cast), 3 rounds of strategy, the opposing advocate plays a mixed strategy shown as percentages, and the jury has a shared "mood" drawn from 5 Gauss–Hermite points. Shown odds integrate over exactly those points; the verdict draws from them. Ties acquit.
- 2026-10-18: Favor of the people and of the boni saturate (each act moves you less as you approach ±100). Dignitas from speeches removed; dignitas comes from birth, rank, offices, service and verdicts.
- 2026-10-18: Slice rule: the player does not die of natural causes (no heirs until Phase 3). He can still die by choice (risky choices with shown odds) and on campaign. NPCs age and die normally.
- 2026-10-18: Historical figures have tendencies, not scripts: events fire on conditions and daily hazards. If a key man is dead, a role may be filled by whoever fits (e.g. the hard-line consul of 121 BC).
- 2026-10-18: Placeholder art: Pixi-drawn district shapes on a plain plan; portraits are colored tablets with initials (lean-colored). No illustration.
- 2026-10-18: GitHub Pages deploy runs from `main` and the working branch via Actions (`deploy.yml`). Pages must be enabled in the repo settings with source "GitHub Actions".
- 2026-10-18 (polish): Opportunities are ordinary content events with `pool: 'opportunity'`. They are not checked daily; the events system offers one, chosen at random among those whose conditions and roles fit, after the player has gone 24 quiet days (7% a day thereafter). Any player-facing event resets the clock. Save version 2 adds `events.lastPrompt`.
- 2026-10-18 (polish): New DSL pieces: `occupy`, `act` (start an action from a choice), `lend`, conditions `leadsCase`, `connected`, numbers `law`, random role picks. Content still needs no engine changes for new opportunities.
- 2026-10-18 (polish): Young men's actions (rhetor, watching the courts, the Campus) are open from 16; a commander's staff from 16 counts as service (half the risk, more of the commander's regard).
- 2026-10-18 (polish): A debt that land can cover ends in a forced sale of land (income lost, a small hit to standing), not bankruptcy. Bankruptcy is for those whose land cannot cover it.
- 2026-10-18 (polish): Election weights: the country tribes weigh the people's favor more (0.013) and the boni's less (0.012); the field of outside candidates is a little larger and stronger. All three bot paths now reach quaestor in roughly the same share of runs.
- 2026-10-18 (polish): Mortality is Gompertz from age 26 (about 0.2%/yr at 20, 2.6% at 50, 7% at 62), with varied causes by age.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): The player now ages and dies like anyone. On death the heir (the son named in the testament, else the eldest son, adopted sons included) becomes the player and inherits money, debts, loans, favors owed, non-kin ties, and a weakened copy of what others remember of his father. No heir: the house is extinct and the game ends. The heir is chosen in advance (Family tab), not at the deathbed.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Exile is a setback: if the exile has a son of 16 or more, play passes to him (half the cash goes with him; the exile lives on at Massilia). Recall is not modelled yet.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Elections are generic over the cursus: tribal assembly (35 tribes) for quaestor, tribune and aedile; centuriate (193 centuries by wealth) for praetor, consul and censor. Minimum ages 27/27/33/39/42/45, quaestorship before aedile/praetor, praetorship before consul, a year out between magistracies, ten years between consulships (Marius gets an exemption flag after Arausio), patricians cannot be tribunes (re-election to the tribunate is legal, as Gaius Gracchus showed, but NPCs rarely try), censors only in census years. A new man standing for consul is handicapped in the rich centuries (−1.6 × the century's wealth, shown).
- 2026-10-18 (3a): History's men are "expected" for an office within two years of their recorded year (fasti now list quaestors and praetors too), which makes them stand and gives them a shown "everyone expects him to win" bonus (+2.0). A crowded field (3× the seats; 2.5× for quaestor/tribune) keeps all but the eager out.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Laws are a new module (`assembly`): a tribune or consul posts a bill from `content/laws.ts`; 24 days later each other tribune is asked in turn whether he vetoes (logistic on politics, opinion, client loyalty, favors, boldness, danger), then the tribes vote (logistic on appeal, the proposer's favor with the people, speeches). The shown chance is the product, and the vote rolls exactly those numbers. A bill that fails waits a year.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Office powers are content events on `office_start_<office>` hooks: aedile's games (modest/generous/borrow for lavish), tribune's veto and danger, praetor presiding over every trial in his year (may tilt the jury by 0.3 logit, with memories), consul's war command next spring (honest win chance), censor's roll of the Senate (strike an enemy, enroll a client). Ex-praetors and ex-consuls are offered a province (light/usual/hard hand); gifts and extortion become evidence when they return.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Bribery (ambitus) by an elected man who spent HS 15,000+ opens a prosecution; conviction fines him and bars him from office for ten years.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Letters: deaths abroad, and any death while the player is abroad, are not logged at once. Gossip reaches Rome at 60% of the letter time; the letter (a player event) arrives after the province's travel time (Sicily 9 days … Asia 35). Abroad, the player hears Rome's news after the same delay.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Timeline runs to the end of 100 BC. New pressures `numidia` and `north` ripen the Jugurthine war and the Cimbric wars; key events (Cirta, Jugurtha in Rome, Suthul, the Mamilian court, Metellus and Marius, the head count, Arausio, Aquae Sextiae, Vercellae, Saturninus and Glaucia) fire on conditions with daily hazards and do not fire if their men are dead.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Performance: caches for the character list, children by parent and the day's election forecast (never stored in state); NPC marriage looks at a few households, not all. A 133–100 BC lifetime takes ~20 s headless.
- 2026-10-18 (3a): Bots run the full 33 years; because a lifetime now takes ~20 s, the baseline in balance.md uses 200 runs per strategy instead of 1000 (≈45 min on 4 cores). Use `--runs 1000` when time allows.
